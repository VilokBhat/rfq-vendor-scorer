VENDOR PROFILE

Company: Arcline Aerospace Components
Location: Hosur, Tamil Nadu
Established: 2016
Employees: 19

CAPABILITIES

Machining of aluminium and mild steel components for aerospace ground support
equipment, tooling and non-flight hardware. Four CNC machines: two 3-axis
vertical machining centres and two CNC lathes. No 5-axis capability; complex
geometries are handled through multi-setup 3-axis work with fixturing.

Demonstrated working tolerance +/-0.05 mm. Surface finish typically Ra 3.2 as
machined; better finishes require outsourced grinding or polishing.

Experience with 6061 aluminium. Limited 7075 experience — two prototype jobs
to date, both under 20 pieces.

QUALITY

AS9100D certified (valid to August 2027).
ISO 9001:2015 certified.

No in-house CMM. Dimensional inspection by height gauge, micrometer and go/no-go
gauges. CMM work is outsourced to a NABL-accredited laboratory in Bangalore,
adding two to three working days per inspection cycle. AS9102 first-article
reports prepared in-house using outsourced measurement data.

Full material traceability to mill test certificate, maintained under the
AS9100 quality system.

CAPACITY AND DELIVERY

Single shift, five days. Current utilisation approximately 90%. Typical lead
time for batches above 100 pieces is 9 to 11 weeks.

REFERENCES

Two aerospace GSE manufacturers. Largest single order to date: 120 pieces.
