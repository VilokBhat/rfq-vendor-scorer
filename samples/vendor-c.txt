WHAT WE DO

Our state-of-the-art facility houses a comprehensive range of modern CNC
equipment capable of meeting the tightest tolerances demanded by today's
aerospace programmes. From complex multi-axis machined components to finished,
treated and fully certified assemblies, we handle it end to end.

We offer a complete suite of surface treatment and special process services,
including conversion coating, anodising and passivation, carried out to
international aerospace specifications. Our raw material division maintains
strong relationships with leading global mills and can source aerospace-grade
alloys in a wide range of forms and specifications.
