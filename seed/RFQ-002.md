# RFQ-002 — Surface Treatment, Chemical Conversion Coating

**Scope:** Chromate conversion coating per MIL-DTL-5541 Type II Class 1A on
aluminium components
**Batch sizes:** 50–400 units
**Turnaround:** 10 working days

## Mandatory

- NADCAP accreditation for Chemical Processing

## Required

- Certificate of conformance per batch
- Salt spray test reports on request
- RoHS-compliant process chemistry

## Preferred

- Co-located with machining capability
- Experience with space-grade hardware
- Ability to handle parts up to 600 mm
