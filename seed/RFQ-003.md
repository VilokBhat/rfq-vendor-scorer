# RFQ-003 — Raw Material Supply, Titanium Sheet and Plate

**Material:** Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5, AMS 4911 sheet
**Thickness range:** 1.0–6.0 mm
**Volume:** approx. 2 tonnes per year, called off against a rate contract

## Mandatory

- Mill test certificates traceable to heat lot

## Required

- ISO 9001 certified
- Ability to supply cut-to-size
- DFARS-compliant sourcing documentation

## Preferred

- Existing stock holding in India
- Prior supply to space or defence programmes
- Consignment stocking arrangement
